More Information

Verify Sender & Domain

Always confirm sender addresses and look for subtle domain typos.

  • • Inspect the full email address (not just the display name).
  • • Watch for homoglyphs and extra subdomains (example.co vs example.com).

Inspect Links Safely

Preview targets before clicking; avoid shortened URLs when unsure.

  • • Hover to preview hrefs or right-click → copy link address.
  • • Paste into a safe editor to inspect the destination without navigating.

Protect Your Accounts

Use unique passwords, 2FA and keep software updated.

  • • Never share OTPs or passwords.
  • • Use a password manager to avoid mistyped domains.
